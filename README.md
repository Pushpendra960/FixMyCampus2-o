**Category:** Generative AI | Automation | Campus Operations

FixMyCampus 2.0 aims to simplify how campus issues are reported, categorized, assigned, and tracked.

**Problem it addresses:**
Traditional campus issue reporting can involve manual communication, delays in identifying the responsible department, and limited visibility into ticket status.

An AI-assisted issue management system that helps organize campus complaints and streamline their routing and tracking.
